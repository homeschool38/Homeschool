Common questions

Before you choose a Name

What age is this for?

The child workbook is designed for young learners with parent support, with activities that can stretch for older children through reflection, copywork, and discussion.

What is the Discovery Pack?

The Discovery Pack includes both the Child Workbook and Parent Guide for one Name, so your child has printable pages and you have teaching support.

Do I need the Parent Guide?

Yes, if you want the full experience. The Parent Guide gives you deeper explanations, teaching cautions, Qur’an and Sunnah references, and discussion support.

Why are only a few Names available?

Collection 1 is being released one Name at a time until the first 33 Names are complete, in shā’ Allāh. You can begin with the available Names now.

Are these physical books?

No. These are digital printable resources delivered by email after checkout, so you can print the pages you need at home.

Can I try before buying?

Yes. Download the free One-Name-a-Week Guide to see how one Name can be taught gently across a homeschool week before purchasing a Discovery Pack.
