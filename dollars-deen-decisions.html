Recommended age and readiness

Best for approximately ages 9–14.

Upper-elementary and middle-school learners benefit most when they can read or discuss short passages, follow multi-step activities, explain their reasoning, and work with simple money amounts.

Important: This is a foundational homeschool curriculum. It is not a fatwa manual, investing course, zakat calculator, or substitute for qualified guidance on modern financial products.
  • Younger learners may use selected pages with stronger parent support.
  • Older learners may extend discussions and complete more activities independently.
  • Parents are repeatedly guided to pause, simplify, investigate, or ask someone qualified.
  • Children are not asked to carry adult financial stress or judge family finances.
  • The curriculum builds awareness, reasoning, habits, and respectful caution.

Common questions

Before you choose a download

Is this one combined PDF?

No. The complete bundle delivers the Child Workbook, Parent Teaching Guide, and Game Pack as separate files so you can print and use only what you need.

Do I need all three resources?

The Child Workbook can stand alone, but the full curriculum is strongest with the Parent Guide and Game Pack because the guide gives teaching support and the pack provides hands-on practice.

Does this teach children to invest?

No. It introduces careful awareness only: children learn that investing may involve risk, business activity matters, gambling is forbidden, and real decisions require parents and qualified guidance.

How is the curriculum delivered?

All products are digital downloads. After checkout, Bitship sends the matching PDF or complete bundle ZIP to the email address used during purchase.

Is this suitable for co-ops?

Yes. Families may use it individually, in a homeschool co-op, or as a structured Islamic studies and life-skills unit.

Is the fake money realistic?

No. The Game Pack uses clearly fictional learning money designed for educational practice, not realistic currency imitation.
