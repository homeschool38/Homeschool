Questions Answered

Before you choose a resource.

What format are the files?

All resources are delivered as PDFs. They are designed to be printed at home, and many pages can also be filled digitally in a PDF reader.

How do I receive my files after purchase?

Your download link is emailed to you after checkout. No waiting and no shipping — just make sure your checkout email is correct.

Are the Names of Allah resources physical books?

No. They are printable digital resources. Each Name is available as a Discovery Pack, Child Workbook, or Parent Guide.

Do I need teaching experience?

No. The resources are written to support real Muslim mothers with clear prompts, guidance, and gentle structure.

Will these work for my homeschool style?

Yes. Whether your style is Charlotte Mason, classical, eclectic, or still forming, the resources are flexible and faith-centered.

Can I share these with friends or in a co-op?

These are licensed for personal household use only. If a sister would love them, please send her the product link instead.
