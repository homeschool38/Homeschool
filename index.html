Questions answered

What format are the files?

All resources are delivered as PDFs. Fillable digitally in Adobe Acrobat or any PDF reader, and designed to be printed at home too.

How do I receive my files after purchase?

Your download link is emailed to you immediately after checkout. No waiting, no shipping — access within minutes, in sha Allah.

What age range are the Prophet Story Scripts for?

The scripts are written for ages 4 to 12, with separate discussion questions and activities for ages 4-7 and 8-12. Perfect for mixed-age homeschool families.

Do I need any teaching experience to use the scripts?

Not at all. Every script is fully written with built-in pause cues — you simply pick it up and read aloud. No preparation, no lesson planning, no Arabic expertise needed.

Will the Workbook and Playbook work for my homeschool style?

Yes, whether you're Charlotte Mason, classical, eclectic, or still figuring it out, the framework is flexible and faith-centred, not method-specific.
